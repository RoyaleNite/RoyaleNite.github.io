Seqwence

A video editor that runs on your own PC: a multi-track timeline, effects, motion tracking, captions and lossless exports.

Desktop video editor · 2026 · Source-available (FSL-1.1-MIT)

What it does

  • A multi-track editor

    Clips, transitions, resizable track lanes, guides with snapping, and a second monitor that shows the clips as they were shot.

  • Effects that export as previewed

    Colour and light, glow, green screen, camera shake, flash, RGB split, zoom punch, plus OpenFX, frei0r and LV2 plugins.

  • Motion tracking

    Draw a box and an AI tracker follows the object, checking every frame against your box; Track & follow keeps it centred.

  • Captions

    Speech to text, including Afrikaans and English mixed, with animated words and text presets.

  • Lossless exports

    Exports on the GPU (NVENC), checked frame by frame against the source.

  • A Windows installer

    Everything included, a first-run setup, and signed updates that always ask first.

How it's made

Seqwence started as a tool to trim and export game clips without losing any quality, and grew into a full editor. The editor runs in a browser window over a local Node server, so projects also open from a phone over your own network.

Exports are built from the same description the live preview draws, so what you see is what is exported, and every lossless export is checked against its source.

  • Node.js
  • JavaScript
  • WebGL
  • FFmpeg
  • Electron
  • ONNX Runtime
  • C++ (OpenFX host)
