I'm Waldre Roos, a freelance software developer in Mbombela, South Africa. I make the software a small business uses every day: the roster, the bookings, the leave forms, the price list, and the report someone used to put together by hand every Friday.

I design it, build it and then host it on my own servers, so when something needs to change there is one person to call, and that person built it. Shifties, my rostering and clock-in product, runs restaurants, guest houses and schools and is billed monthly in rand.

Everything is made for the phone in your pocket first, because that is where your staff will use it. Tell me what yours runs on.
