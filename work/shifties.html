Shifties

Staff rostering, clock-in, leave and bookings for restaurants, guest houses and schools, billed monthly in rand.

Rostering & bookings · 2026 · Closed, a commercial product · shifties.co.za

What it does

  • Shift Scatter

    Builds the week's roster for you within a wage budget, and respects leave, days off, half days, overtime, minimum staff and rest between shifts.

  • Clock-in

    An installable clock-in app and a kiosk mode for a shared tablet: staff clock in with their ID, no login needed.

  • Leave and closures

    Leave requests with approval, public holidays and closures that the roster already knows about.

  • Bookings

    A public booking page and a bookings screen for the team.

  • Budget and forecast

    A turnover target, the wage cost so far and a forecast of where the period will end.

  • Schools

    A schools mode with timetables, teachers, subjects and relief cover.

How it's made

Shifties is a product I design, build, host and support. A business signs up, adds its people and shifts, and has a roster the same afternoon; there is a demo with a seeded restaurant to try first.

The front end is React and TypeScript; the API is Django with Celery for the background work (roster generation, reminders, reports), PostgreSQL, and Docker, deployed from CI.

  • React 19
  • TypeScript
  • Vite
  • Tailwind
  • Django
  • Django REST
  • Celery
  • Redis
  • PostgreSQL
  • Docker
