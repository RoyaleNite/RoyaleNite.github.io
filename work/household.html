Household

Shared shopping lists, a grocery price finder across South African retailers, a budget and a meal planner.

Home & money app · 2026 · Private, made for one household

One search, every shop's price.

What it does

  • A grocery price finder

    Searches Checkers, Shoprite, Pick n Pay, Woolworths, SPAR, Clicks, Dis-Chem and Takealot at once, matches the same product across shops, and compares pack sizes.

  • Specials and leaflets

    This week's specials and the shops' leaflets, in the app.

  • Shared shopping lists

    Grouped by shop, with favourites and the best price next to each item.

  • A budget

    Expenses split per person and per period, with amounts that can be hidden.

  • A meal planner

    Meals and recipes scaled to the household, costed from live prices, and sent to the shopping list.

  • Deploys that roll themselves back

    Every release runs the tests and checks the live site; if anything fails, the old version is put back automatically.

How it's made

Household is a private app for one family, so there is nothing to sign up for. Its price finder is the interesting part: each shop has its own adapter, and the results are normalised so a 2 kg bag and two 1 kg bags compare fairly.

It is a Next.js app on SQLite, with Playwright driving a headless browser for the shops that need one, tests in Vitest, and a deploy script that snapshots the database first.

  • React 19
  • TypeScript

  • SQLite
  • Playwright
