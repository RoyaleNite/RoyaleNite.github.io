Fit

An installable training log: a gym timer, streaks, a year heatmap, weight, sleep and food, and training groups.

Training log app · 2026 · Closed · fit.waldre.co.za

A year of training, one square a day.

What it does

  • Workouts

    A live session screen with a timer and supersets, or just "I went"; plans you can share.

  • Streaks and a year heatmap

    Every training day on one grid, with progress charts per exercise, goals and challenges.

  • Food and water

    A food diary with macros, barcode scanning with an Open Food Facts lookup, kJ or kcal, and one-tap water.

  • An exercise catalogue

    About 250 exercises with pictures and demonstrations, and search by describing the movement.

  • Friends, groups and gyms

    Add friends by tag and train together, with reminders sent as push notifications.

  • An admin console

    Users, the catalogue, gyms, patch notes and email settings, all in the app.

How it's made

Fit is a progressive web app: it installs from the browser on Android and iPhone and works like a native app, with push reminders.

It is a Next.js app with Prisma on SQLite, its own session login, and Web Push, hosted on my own server.

  • Next.js 16
  • TypeScript
  • Tailwind
  • Prisma
  • SQLite
  • Web Push
  • Recharts
